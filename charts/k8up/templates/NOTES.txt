#####################
! Attention !
#####################

This Helm chart includes the K8up CRDs in its crds/ directory.
Helm installs them on the first `helm install`, but it never upgrades or deletes CRDs.
When you upgrade K8up, apply the CRDs of the new version yourself:

  kubectl apply --server-side -f https://github.com/k8up-io/k8up/releases/download/{{ .Values.image.tag }}/k8up-crd.yaml
